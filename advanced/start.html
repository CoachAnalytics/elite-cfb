What each tab is for

  • Heisman WatchWho should win it and who actually will, which are rarely the same ten names.
  • Model vs MarketWhere our numbers and the betting market disagree, with every disagreement graded.
  • PaceWho plays fast, how often, and whether the speed actually suits them.
  • Team ProfileEvery number one team has, ranked against the other 67, best and worst first.
  • MatchupAny two teams, one side of the ball against the other, with the shapes that show where the game is won.
  • StrengthsWhat a team is good and bad at, decided by the numbers rather than by reputation.
  • Chart BuilderAny two measures against each other, every team as its own logo, saved as a picture worth posting.
  • Red ZoneInside the 20: who finishes drives with touchdowns and who settles for three.
  • Short YardageA yard to go, or two: who gets it, how they get it, and against whom.
  • Third DownThird down by distance, and what each one is worth - short is a different sport from long.
  • Early DownsFirst and second down, where a drive is decided before anyone notices.
  • Second HalfWho comes out of the tunnel different, with a lead and from behind.
  • Fourth QuarterThe last quarter, and the one-score games inside it.
  • What HappensThe snap after a moment: an explosive play, a stuffed run, a third down converted.
  • Drive AnalysisDrives rather than plays: how they start, how far they go, what they are worth.
  • Player ComparisonTwo players side by side, the same questions asked of both, rates before totals.
  • Player SplitsA player's season cut by down, distance and field position.
