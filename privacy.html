Privacy policy

What is collected, who else sees it, and how to be rid of it.

Last updated 11 October 2026.

The short version

An email address so you can sign in, a payment record held by Stripe, and a count of page views with no cookies attached. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared beyond the handful of services named below that are needed to run the site.

What is collected

Your email address and password, to create an account and sign you in. The password is stored only as a cryptographic hash, which means it cannot be read back - not by us either.

Your subscription status: whether a subscription is live, which plan, and when the current period ends.

Payment details are entered on Stripe's own checkout page and are held by Stripe. Card numbers never reach this site and are never stored by us.

Page views, counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and does not follow anyone between sites. It records that a page was viewed, roughly where from, and how quickly it loaded.

Messages you send through the contact page: your name, email address and what you wrote, so a reply is possible.

What is not collected

No profile of you is built here, and nothing about you is sold to data brokers.

Advertising

The free pages carry advertising from Google AdSense. The subscriber section carries none, and that is enforced when the site is built rather than left to whoever edits a page next.

Google and its partners use cookies and device identifiers to choose and measure those adverts, and may use what you have done on other sites to decide which ones you see. That is Google's doing, under Google's policies, and you can review or switch off personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com or opt out of third-party cookies at optout.aboutads.info.

Visitors in the UK and the EEA are asked for consent before any of it runs, and advertising cookies are not set unless that consent is given.

Your name, your email address, your payment details and anything about your subscription are never passed to Google or to any advertiser.

Who else is involved

Supabase holds accounts and subscription records. Stripe handles payments. Cloudflare serves the site and counts page views. Resend delivers email, such as a confirmation or a password reset. Google serves the advertising on the free pages. Each is used only for its own job, and each has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and what is stored in your browser

A sign-in token, so you stay signed in. Small conveniences such as the last team you looked at or a chart you built, stored in your own browser and never sent anywhere. On the free pages, Google's advertising cookies, where you have consented to them.

How long anything is kept

Account and subscription records are kept while the account exists, and for as long afterwards as tax and accounting rules require for the payment records. Analytics are aggregate and are not tied to you.

Your choices

You may ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for your account and personal data to be deleted. Payment records that must be kept for accounting are the one exception, and they are kept only as long as required.

Ask through the contact page or at support@fbcoachanalytics.com, and allow a few days for a reply.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.
